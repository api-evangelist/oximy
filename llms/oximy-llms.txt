# Oximy

> Oximy is an AI adoption and governance platform — "the AI adoption engine for every company." It gives organizations visibility into how their people and applications use AI tools, and enforces usage policy. For developers, the Oximy Public API and official SDK provide zero-overhead LLM observability (telemetry) and inline policy enforcement (PII redaction, prompt-injection detection, deny/allow lists, token/cost/rate limits) that wrap any OpenAI-compatible client.

Backed by Y Combinator (W26). This llms.txt was GENERATED by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the company's public surface and the official SDK.
